Why Health-Care Affordability Is Becoming a Bigger Concern for Older Households

New research shows that paying for needed health care remains a major concern for Americans, including people who have coverage.

Gallup reported that fewer than half of Americans said they could afford needed care and medications, a five-year low in its 2026 polling. Separate Commonwealth Fund research found that older Americans on Medicare can still face higher out-of-pocket spending and cost-related access problems than older adults in peer countries.

What to keep in mind

  • Review your plan's annual out-of-pocket exposure.
  • Ask about generic or lower-cost medication options.
  • Use preventive services that your coverage includes.
  • Keep a list of bills and explanation-of-benefits statements.
  • Ask for written estimates before major non-emergency care when possible.
A useful rule: Slow down when a headline asks you to act immediately. Verify the source, check the date and look for the original agency, research paper or provider information.

The bigger picture

Insurance rules and costs are personal. These findings describe population-level trends, not an individual's coverage.
