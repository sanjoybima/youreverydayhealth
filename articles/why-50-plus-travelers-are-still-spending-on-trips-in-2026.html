Why 50-Plus Travelers Are Still Spending on Trips in 2026

Travel remains a priority for many older adults even as costs rise, with a growing focus on value, meaning and accessibility.

AARP Research reports that 64% of adults 50-plus expect to take at least one leisure trip in 2026 and that expected travel spending rose to $7,292. The trend is less about traveling at any price and more about choosing trips that feel worth the money.

What to keep in mind

  • Compare the total trip cost, not just airfare.
  • Look for flexible cancellation terms.
  • Ask hotels about accessibility before booking.
  • Build rest days into longer itineraries.
  • Buy travel insurance only after comparing what it actually covers.
A useful rule: Slow down when a headline asks you to act immediately. Verify the source, check the date and look for the original agency, research paper or provider information.

The bigger picture

Travel costs and availability change. Check the provider's current terms before booking.
