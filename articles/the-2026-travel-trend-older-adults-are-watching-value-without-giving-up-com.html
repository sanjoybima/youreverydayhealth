The 2026 Travel Trend Older Adults Are Watching: Value Without Giving Up Comfort

Travelers are looking for ways to control costs without turning a relaxing trip into a stressful one.

AARP's latest travel research shows that adults 50-plus continue to prioritize travel while adapting to cost pressures. One practical strategy is to spend more on the parts of a trip that affect comfort—location, bed quality, transportation—and save on less important extras.

What to keep in mind

  • Travel in shoulder seasons when practical.
  • Compare hotel location against transportation costs.
  • Use refundable rates when the price difference is reasonable.
  • Choose fewer destinations for longer stays.
  • Set a daily spending ceiling before departure.
A useful rule: Slow down when a headline asks you to act immediately. Verify the source, check the date and look for the original agency, research paper or provider information.

The bigger picture

Travel decisions are personal. Always compare current prices and cancellation rules.
