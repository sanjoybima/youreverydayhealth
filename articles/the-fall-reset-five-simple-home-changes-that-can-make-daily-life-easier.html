The Fall Reset: Five Simple Home Changes That Can Make Daily Life Easier

Autumn is a good time to make small home changes before winter makes maintenance more difficult.

A home does not have to look like a medical facility to be easier to live in. Small improvements can reduce daily friction: better lighting, clear walking paths, easy-to-reach storage, safer bathroom surfaces and simple emergency contacts. These changes can also help visitors and family members.

What to keep in mind

  • Clear clutter from stairs and hallways.
  • Improve lighting near entrances.
  • Move everyday items to comfortable heights.
  • Check smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms.
  • Keep emergency contacts visible.
A useful rule: Slow down when a headline asks you to act immediately. Verify the source, check the date and look for the original agency, research paper or provider information.

The bigger picture

Every home and mobility situation is different. Use professional advice for major accessibility changes.
