Social Security's 2027 COLA Is Coming: What Retirees Should Do Before the Announcement

The annual Social Security cost-of-living adjustment is determined in October, making this a useful month for retirement planning.

The annual COLA is based on a statutory formula. Media forecasts can provide context, but they are not final. Instead of planning around a headline number, retirees can prepare a simple range: current income, a modest increase scenario and a higher increase scenario. This keeps the budget useful even when the final number changes.

What to keep in mind

  • Build a low, middle and high income scenario.
  • Review Medicare and tax assumptions separately.
  • Do not change investment allocations because of a single COLA forecast.
  • Check SSA's official announcement when released.
  • Use your actual spending history as the foundation.
A useful rule: Slow down when a headline asks you to act immediately. Verify the source, check the date and look for the original agency, research paper or provider information.

The bigger picture

This is general planning information, not personalized retirement advice.
