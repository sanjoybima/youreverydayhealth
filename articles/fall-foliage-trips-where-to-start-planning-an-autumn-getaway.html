Fall Foliage Trips: Where to Start Planning an Autumn Getaway

Peak leaf color is moving across the country, making fall a strong season for slower-paced road trips and scenic getaways.

The Old Farmer's Almanac publishes a 2026 fall foliage map and regional forecast. For travelers over 55, fall trips can work especially well when the itinerary is built around shorter drives, comfortable lodging and one or two main activities each day.

What to keep in mind

  • Check local peak-color forecasts before choosing dates.
  • Avoid packing too many stops into one day.
  • Book lodging near the main area you want to explore.
  • Check walking distances and terrain.
  • Keep weather changes in mind.
A useful rule: Slow down when a headline asks you to act immediately. Verify the source, check the date and look for the original agency, research paper or provider information.

The bigger picture

Foliage forecasts are estimates. Actual color depends on local weather.
