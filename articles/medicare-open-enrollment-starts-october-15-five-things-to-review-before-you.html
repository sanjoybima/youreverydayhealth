Medicare Open Enrollment Starts October 15: Five Things to Review Before You Choose

Medicare's annual Open Enrollment period runs from October 15 through December 7, making October a useful time to review coverage.

Medicare.gov lists October 15 as the start of Open Enrollment. During this period, people can review or change certain Medicare health and drug coverage for the following year. The best plan is not necessarily the one with the lowest premium: provider networks, prescription coverage, deductibles, copays and out-of-pocket limits all matter.

What to keep in mind

  • Check your current medications against the plan's formulary.
  • Confirm that your preferred doctors and hospitals are in network.
  • Compare total annual costs, not just monthly premiums.
  • Review pharmacy options and mail-order pricing.
  • Use Medicare.gov or a trusted counselor for plan-specific questions.
A useful rule: Slow down when a headline asks you to act immediately. Verify the source, check the date and look for the original agency, research paper or provider information.

The bigger picture

Plan availability and benefits vary by location. Always check your specific plan documents before enrolling.
