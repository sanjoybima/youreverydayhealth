2027 Social Security COLA: Why October Is the Month to Watch

The Social Security Administration determines annual cost-of-living adjustments each October. Forecasts are not the same thing as the final number.

SSA explains that COLAs are determined each October under formulas set by law. Several private forecasts have circulated ahead of the official announcement, but a forecast should not be treated as a guaranteed increase. For retirees, the useful question is how any increase would interact with Medicare premiums, taxes and actual household expenses.

What to keep in mind

  • Wait for the official SSA announcement before changing a budget.
  • Separate a benefit increase from the amount that reaches your bank account.
  • Review Medicare premiums and taxes separately.
  • Use your actual spending history to build a 2027 budget.
  • Be skeptical of posts promising a specific 'guaranteed' check increase.
A useful rule: Slow down when a headline asks you to act immediately. Verify the source, check the date and look for the original agency, research paper or provider information.

The bigger picture

Forecasts cited in the media can change. The official COLA is determined by SSA under federal law.
