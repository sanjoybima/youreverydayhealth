# Your Everyday Health

Static editorial website created for a U.S. 55+ audience.

## Included
- Home page
- 6 category pages
- 30 article pages (5 per category)
- Contact page with contact form
- Disclaimer
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Responsive CSS
- robots.txt
- sitemap.xml

## Email
info@youreverydayhealth.com

## Important production note
The contact form in this static package uses `mailto:`. For reliable form submissions, connect it to a secure form provider or your own backend before launch.

## Content note
Current-news pieces are dated October 8, 2026 and link to their source. Forecasts are labeled as forecasts. Health/financial content is general information, not professional advice.
