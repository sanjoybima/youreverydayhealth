# Your Everyday Health

Static editorial website created for a U.S. 55+ audience.

## Included
- Home page
- 5 category pages: Health, Money, Retirement, Lifestyle, Travel
- 25 article pages (5 per category)
- Editorial 16:9 header image for every article
- Header images include short hooks and are designed to be reusable as matching Taboola ad creatives
- Contact page with contact form
- Disclaimer
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Responsive CSS
- robots.txt
- sitemap.xml

## Email
info@youreverydayhealth.com

## Important production note
The contact form in this static package uses `mailto:`. For reliable form submissions, connect it to a secure form provider or your own backend before launch.

## Content note
The site focuses on health, money, retirement, lifestyle and travel. Health/financial content is general information, not professional advice.
